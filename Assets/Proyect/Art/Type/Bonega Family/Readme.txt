Bonega is a display font inspired by classic typography on ancient stone inscriptions. Consist of 4 weight (light, regular, bold, black) with each matching italics.

With sharp and manly characteristics, Bonega family is suitable for powerful headlines, logotypes, beautiful signs, posters and more. Contain more than 400 glyphs including stylistic sets that make your typographic design more attractive.
